# 🤖 AI Models — AI Resume Analyzer

The **AI Resume Analyzer** uses AI-powered analysis to evaluate resumes, identify relevant skills, detect missing skills, and provide personalized recommendations for improving a resume.

This document explains the AI model integration and the resume analysis workflow.

---

## 🧠 Available AI Model

### 1. Google Gemini

**Google Gemini** is used to provide AI-powered resume analysis and recommendations.

Depending on the configured application workflow, Gemini can help with:

- Resume content analysis
- Resume quality assessment
- Skill identification
- Missing skill identification
- Target-role relevance analysis
- Resume improvement recommendations
- Strength and weakness analysis
- Personalized career suggestions
- Resume scoring

The AI analysis is designed to help users understand how well their resume matches their selected career role and where improvements can be made.
